Permanently erase imported data?

The selected source history, original exports, attachments, and authorization retained by this host will be removed. This cannot be undone here. Copies outside this host are unaffected.

Keep a recovery copy

Save your archive and account keys in an encrypted file that can be recovered without this host. Use a separate passphrase and keep it somewhere safe. Anyone with both the file and passphrase can access the archive and act as your Tangled account.

Recover offline with the Tangled recovery command documented in the social imports guide. Your source app login sessions are excluded.
