# Loomed

A sealed radio set for Tangled circles. Not a chat SaaS column of bubbles.

You generate or import a `did:sol` identity, point at your knot, and keep a local replica. Text and images travel as `pub.tangled.chat`. Read receipts are `pub.loomed.receipt`. Search is local. If the knot is down, history stays and a copper banner says so.

First run: onboard (generate / import JSON). Then the band: conversation rail, message log, transmit at the bottom.
